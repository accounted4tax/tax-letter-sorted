VAT late submission penalty points and the £200 penalty explained

Since 2023, each VAT Return sent late gets a penalty point. When you reach your threshold, HMRC charges a £200 penalty – and another £200 for every late return after that.

How the points system works

Every VAT Return that reaches HMRC after its deadline earns one penalty point – even a nil return, or one where HMRC owes you money. A point on its own costs nothing. The money starts when you reach the threshold for how often you file:

You filePenalty threshold
Annually2 points
Quarterly4 points
Monthly5 points

When you reach the threshold, HMRC charges a £200 penalty. While you stay at the threshold, every further late return costs another £200.

Getting points removed

Below the threshold, each point expires on its own after about 2 years (the end of the month 24 months after the month the late return was due).

At the threshold, all your points are wiped only when you have both:

  • sent every return on time for a set period – 12 months if you file quarterly (4 returns), 24 months if annually, 6 months if monthly
  • sent any returns still outstanding from the last 24 months

So the most important thing is to keep sending returns on time, even while you challenge a penalty.

Late payment is separate

Paying VAT late is dealt with separately, with its own late payment penalties and interest. A late return and a late payment can happen for the same period.

Can you challenge a point or penalty?

Yes. The penalty letter offers a review. You can challenge a point or the £200 if it is wrong, or if you had a reasonable excuse – something unexpected or outside your control, such as a serious illness, a death in the family shortly before the deadline, or a failure of your software or HMRC's systems. If the excuse is accepted, the point is removed too.

How to ask for a review

  1. Act within 30 days of the date on the penalty decision.
  2. Ask for a review in the business's VAT online account, or write to: Solicitor's Office and Legal Services, HMRC, BX9 1ZT – or the address on your letter.
  3. Explain what happened, with dates, and say what evidence you have.

Questions

Do I get a VAT penalty point for a nil return?

Yes. For VAT periods starting on or after 1 January 2023, nil returns and repayment returns sent late also get a penalty point.

How many points before I get a £200 VAT penalty?

2 points if you file annually, 4 if you file quarterly and 5 if you file monthly. At the threshold, each late return costs £200.

How do I get rid of VAT penalty points?

Below the threshold they expire after about 2 years. At the threshold, you need to send every return on time for a set period – 12 months for quarterly filers – and have no returns outstanding.
