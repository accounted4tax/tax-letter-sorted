3. How long we keep it

  • Free explanation only: the letter and everything read from it are deleted automatically within 24 hours, or within 30 days if you continue to the questions or ask us to call you.
  • £19 self-serve appeal: the letter, your answers and the appeal PDF are deleted automatically 14 days after we email you the appeal. We keep your name, email, phone, what you bought, the payment reference, when you approved the appeal and your marketing choice for 6 years, because accounting law requires us to keep sales records.
  • Accountant review (£39 / £59) and services we provide as your accountant: we keep the case and work records for 5 years after the end of the tax year they relate to. Identity documents and the director's confirmation are kept for 5 years after our work for you ends, as the Money Laundering Regulations require. Health information is kept only as part of the appeal itself.
  • Help, call-back and quote requests: up to 12 months, unless you become a client.
  • Our client records: if you choose an accountant review, an appointment, or ask us for a quote or a service, we keep a copy of your case record and documents (including identity documents) in our private, secure cloud storage, as part of our practice records. If you do not go ahead, the copy is set aside after 30 days and deleted within 12 months. If you become a client, it is kept for the periods above.
  • Email delivery log: 6 years.

4. Who processes your data for us

We use these providers. Each works under a written contract and only on our instructions.

  • Cloud hosting provider – our database, file storage and server code.
  • Cloud storage and office software provider – our private copy of client records, documents and identity documents.
  • Artificial intelligence (AI) provider – reads the letter and writes the plain-language explanation. It does not use our data to train its models and deletes it within 30 days (longer only if required by law or to investigate misuse).
  • Card payment provider – takes card payments. It is also a controller of payment data under its own privacy policy.
  • Email provider – sends our emails.
  • Website host and font provider – show you the web pages; like any website, they receive your device's IP address.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires (for example HMRC, the National Crime Agency or our regulator, AAT) or when you ask us to send an appeal for you.

5. Transfers outside the UK

Some of our providers may process data outside the UK, including in the USA. Where they do, the transfer is protected by the UK's approved safeguards (UK adequacy regulations, or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses). 6. Cookies and similar

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The pages keep a small amount of information in your browser tab (session storage) so that each step of your case works, and your language choice. It is removed when you close the tab.
