Companies House late filing penalty: amounts and how to appeal

Companies House charges a penalty when a company's annual accounts arrive after the deadline. It is not HMRC – and it is harder to appeal than an HMRC penalty.

This letter is from Companies House

Companies House is the register of companies. It is separate from HMRC, so this penalty is for your annual accounts, not your company tax return. If HMRC has charged a penalty for the tax return, see Corporation Tax late filing penalty.

Can you appeal?

Only in exceptional circumstances – Companies House has very little discretion. An example it gives is a fire that destroyed the company's records a few days before the deadline. Other cases that may succeed are a serious illness or death of a director at a critical time when no one else could file, or a Companies House system failure on the day.

Companies House says these are not accepted on their own:

  • the company is dormant, or these were its first accounts
  • the company can't afford to pay, or is in financial difficulty
  • the accountant was ill, or the directors relied on the accountant
  • the directors didn't know the rules, or were living or travelling abroad
  • another director was responsible for the accounts
  • the accounts were delayed or lost in the post

How to appeal

  1. Appeal online through Companies House's appeal service, or by email to enquiries@companieshouse.gov.uk. Include the penalty reference and company number.
  2. Explain what happened, with dates, and attach evidence.
  3. If the appeal is refused, you can ask the Senior Casework Unit to look again, and after that the Independent Adjudicators.

There is no fixed time limit, but appeal straight away – unpaid penalties are passed to debt collectors and can go to the County Court (or the Sheriff Court in Scotland). If you can't pay in one go, you can ask Companies House for monthly instalments.

Questions

Is a Companies House penalty the same as an HMRC penalty?

No. Companies House fines the company for late accounts; HMRC fines it separately for a late company tax return. You may need to deal with both.

My company is dormant – do I still have to pay?

Yes. Being dormant is not a reason Companies House accepts. Dormant companies still have to file accounts on time.

Can I pay a Companies House penalty in instalments?

You can ask. Contact Companies House, preferably by email, and explain why you can't pay the full amount at once.
