2. What the service is – and what it isn't

  • Tax Letter Sorted explains letters from HMRC and Companies House in plain language and helps you prepare a penalty appeal.
  • The free explanation and the £19 self-serve appeal are self-help tools, not personal tax advice. If your situation is complicated, we will suggest an accountant review.
  • The appeal is built only from the facts you give and confirm. We never invent or suggest a reason.
  • HMRC or Companies House decide every appeal. We cannot promise that a penalty will be cancelled.
  • Explanations in other languages are translations. The English text, and the English appeal sent to HMRC or Companies House, are the ones that count.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Give true and complete information and check the appeal carefully before you approve it. Giving false information to HMRC can lead to further penalties.
  • Keep to the deadlines, normally 30 days from the date of the penalty notice. Where you send the appeal yourself, you are responsible for signing it, sending it on time and keeping proof of posting.
  • Paying the penalty or tax is still your responsibility while an appeal is considered, unless HMRC tells you otherwise.
