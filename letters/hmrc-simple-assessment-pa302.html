HMRC Simple Assessment letter (PA302): what it is and how to pay

A Simple Assessment is a tax bill HMRC works out for you, without you filing a tax return. It usually comes when you owe tax that can't be collected through your tax code.

Why you got a Simple Assessment

HMRC uses Simple Assessment when you owe tax but don't need to file a tax return. The usual reasons are:

  • you owe tax that can't be taken automatically through your tax code
  • you owe HMRC more than £3,000

The letter (PA302) shows the income HMRC used – pay, pensions, savings interest, benefits – and how the tax was worked out.

When and how to pay

  • If the letter was sent before 31 October following the tax year, pay by 31 January.
  • If it was sent on or after 31 October, pay within 3 months of the date on the letter.

You can pay online, by bank transfer or by cheque, in one go or in smaller amounts – as long as it's all paid by the deadline. Always use the 14-character payment reference starting with X from your letter. A wrong reference can delay the payment or put it against a different bill.

If you can't pay in full, contact HMRC before the deadline to ask about a payment plan. Interest is charged on anything paid late.

If you think the figures are wrong

Check each income figure against your P60, pension statements and bank interest statements. If something is wrong, contact HMRC within 60 days of the date on the letter – by phone or in writing – and say which amounts are wrong and what they should be.

Keep in mind that you still have to pay by the deadline while HMRC looks at it, unless HMRC tells you in writing that you can wait. If HMRC disagrees with you, it sends a decision letter, and you then have 30 days to appeal.

Simple Assessment and Self Assessment are different

With Simple Assessment, HMRC does the calculation and you just check and pay. With Self Assessment, you file a tax return and work out the tax yourself. If you're already in Self Assessment, you won't usually get a Simple Assessment for the same year.

Questions

Do I need to file a tax return if I got a Simple Assessment?

No. HMRC has already worked out the tax. You only need to check the figures and pay by the deadline.

Can I pay a Simple Assessment in instalments?

You can pay in smaller amounts as long as everything is paid by the deadline. If you need longer, ask HMRC about a payment plan before the deadline.
