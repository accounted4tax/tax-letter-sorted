3. How long we keep it

  • Free explanation only: the letter and everything read from it are deleted automatically within 24 hours, or within 30 days if you continue to the questions or ask us to call you.
  • £19 self-serve appeal: the letter, your answers and the appeal PDF are deleted automatically 14 days after we email you the appeal. We keep your name, email, phone, what you bought, the payment reference, when you approved the appeal and your marketing choice for 6 years, because accounting law requires us to keep sales records.
  • Accountant review (£39 / £59) and services we provide as your accountant: we keep the case, the identity documents and the director's confirmation for 5 years after our work for you ends, as the Money Laundering Regulations require. Health information is kept only as part of the appeal itself.
  • Help, call-back and quote requests: up to 12 months, unless you become a client.
  • Email delivery log: 6 years.

4. Who processes your data for us

We use these providers. Each works under a written contract and only on our instructions.

  • Supabase – database, file storage and server code, hosted in London (UK).
  • Anthropic (Claude) – reads the letter and writes the plain-language explanation. Anthropic does not use our data to train its models and deletes it within 30 days (longer only if required by law or to investigate misuse). Its servers may be in the USA – see section 5.
  • Stripe – card payments. Stripe is also a controller of payment data under its own privacy policy.
  • Brevo (France, EU) – sends our emails.
  • GitHub Pages – hosts the web pages; like any website host it receives your device's IP address.
  • Google Fonts – supplies the typefaces on the pages; it receives your device's IP address.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires (for example HMRC, the National Crime Agency or our regulator, AAT) or when you ask us to send an appeal for you.

5. Transfers outside the UK

Some providers (Anthropic, Stripe, Google, GitHub) may process data in the USA. We rely on the UK's adequacy regulations for organisations certified under the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, or on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Cookies and similar

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The pages keep a small amount of information in your browser tab (session storage) so that each step of your case works, and your language choice. It is removed when you close the tab.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or move it to another provider. You can withdraw your consent (health information, marketing) at any time. Email letters@accounted4tax.co.uk; we reply within one month. Some data we must keep by law (for example anti-money-laundering records) even if you ask us to delete it – we will tell you if that applies.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint · 0303 123 1113.

8. Security

Your files are stored privately, are never public, and links to them expire after a short time. Your case has its own private link; please do not share it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Important changes will be shown on the website.
