HMRC P800 tax calculation letter: refund or tax to pay?

A P800 is HMRC's end-of-year check of the tax you paid through your job or pension. It tells you whether you paid too much – and get a refund – or too little, and how you'll pay it back.

Why you got a P800

After the tax year ends, HMRC compares the tax taken from your pay or pension with the tax you should have paid. If they don't match, it sends a P800. Common reasons are:

  • you were on the wrong tax code
  • you had two jobs (or a job and a pension) paid in the same month
  • you started getting a workplace pension
  • you got Employment and Support Allowance or Jobseeker's Allowance

If you are in Self Assessment, you won't usually get a P800 – your tax is sorted out through your tax return instead.

If the P800 says you're due a refund

  • Letter says you can claim online: claim through GOV.UK, your personal tax account or the HMRC app. You'll need the P800 reference and your National Insurance number. Money usually arrives within 5 working days.
  • Letter says a cheque will be sent: you don't need to do anything. It should arrive within 14 days of the date on the letter.

Watch out for scams. HMRC doesn't ask for bank details by text or email. Only claim through GOV.UK or the official HMRC app – never through a link in a message.

If the P800 says you owe tax

If you still have a job or pension, earn enough, and owe less than £3,000, HMRC usually collects it automatically by changing your tax code. The amount is spread over 12 months, starting the next tax year. You can choose to pay it all at once instead.

If it can't be collected this way, HMRC writes to tell you how to pay – often with a Simple Assessment bill. More about Simple Assessment.

If you think the P800 is wrong

Check the income, benefits and tax figures against your P60, P45 or payslips. If something is wrong, contact HMRC's Income Tax helpline, say which amounts are wrong and what they should be. If HMRC agrees, it sends a new calculation; if not, it explains why.

A wrong tax code often causes the same problem the next year too, so it's worth checking your current code at the same time.

Questions

Is a P800 refund genuine or a scam?

A real P800 comes by post or appears in your personal tax account. Claim only through GOV.UK or the HMRC app. HMRC doesn't send texts or emails asking for bank details.

How long does a P800 refund take?

About 5 working days if you claim online. If the letter says a cheque is being sent, it should arrive within 14 days of the letter.

Do I have to pay the underpayment straight away?

Usually not. If you owe less than £3,000 and have a job or pension, HMRC normally collects it through your tax code over the next tax year. You can pay it in full earlier if you want.
