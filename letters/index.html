Guides

£100 late filing penaltyHMRC charges a fixed £100 penalty when your Self Assessment tax return arrives after the deadline. It applies even if you owe no tax, or paid your tax on time. Daily, 6 and 12-month penaltiesIf your Self Assessment return is more than 3 months late, HMRC adds more penalties on top of the £100: £10 a day, then 5% or £300 at 6 months, and again at 12 months. Late payment penaltyIf tax from your Self Assessment return is still unpaid 30 days after the payment deadline, HMRC charges a penalty of 5% of the unpaid tax – and again at 6 and 12 months. Corporation Tax penaltyIf your company tax return (CT600) reaches HMRC after the filing deadline, HMRC charges the company a penalty. For returns due on or after 1 April 2026, the fixed penalties have doubled. Companies House penaltyCompanies House charges a penalty when a company's annual accounts arrive after the deadline. It is not HMRC – and it is harder to appeal than an HMRC penalty. Nudge letters (online sellers)A 'nudge' letter is HMRC asking you to check your tax, because it has information that you may have income you haven't told it about – often from selling online. It is not a fine and not an investigation, but don't ignore it.
