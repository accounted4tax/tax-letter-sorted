CIS late return penalty for contractors: amounts and appeals

If you're a contractor in the Construction Industry Scheme, your monthly CIS return must reach HMRC by the 19th of the following month. If it's late, HMRC charges a penalty that grows the longer it stays late.

Why you got the penalty

A contractor has to send a CIS return for every tax month (6th to 5th), by the 19th of the next month. For example, the return for 6 May to 5 June is due by 19 June. The penalty is for the return arriving late – it applies even if the deductions were paid on time.

How the penalties grow

Return late byPenalty
1 day£100
2 months£200
6 months£300 or 5% of the CIS deductions on the return, whichever is higher
12 months£300 or 5% of the CIS deductions, whichever is higher

Returns later than that can get an extra penalty of up to £3,000 or 100% of the deductions. Each month is a separate return, so several late months mean several penalties.

No subcontractors paid that month?

You still need to tell HMRC. Either send a nil return, or tell HMRC you've stopped using subcontractors for now (an inactivity request). A missing nil return gets the same penalties as any other.

Can you appeal?

Yes, if the penalty is wrong or you had a reasonable excuse – something unexpected or outside your control, such as a serious illness, a death in the family shortly before the deadline, or a failure of HMRC's online service – and you sent the return as soon as you could.

How to appeal

  1. Appeal within 30 days of the date on the penalty notice.
  2. Use HMRC's online service, or write to the address on the notice (CIS general enquiries). You can't appeal by phone.
  3. Quote your UTR and the payment reference from the notice, and explain what happened, with dates.

Questions

When is the CIS return due?

By the 19th of the month after the tax month ends. The tax month runs from the 6th to the 5th.

Do I need to send a CIS return if I paid no subcontractors?

Yes – send a nil return, or tell HMRC you're temporarily inactive. Otherwise you can be charged the late return penalties.

Can I appeal a CIS penalty by phone?

No. Use HMRC's online service or write to HMRC, quoting your UTR and the payment reference from the notice.
