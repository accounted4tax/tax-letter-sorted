HMRC Self Assessment statement: payments on account and what you owe

A Self Assessment statement shows what you owe HMRC and what you've paid: the balancing payment, payments on account, interest and any penalties. Many people are surprised by the payments on account.

Why you owe more than your tax bill

Most people in Self Assessment pay tax in advance, in two payments on account. Each one is normally half of last year's bill, due on 31 January and 31 July. On 31 January you pay the balancing payment for the year just ended and the first payment on account for the current year – so the first bill can be up to one and a half times what you expected.

You don't have to make payments on account if last year's bill was under £1,000, or if more than 80% of your tax was already paid through your tax code or taken off savings interest.

Reading the statement

  • Balancing payment: the tax for the last year minus any payments on account you made towards it.
  • Payments on account: the advance payments for the current year.
  • Interest: charged on anything paid late, from the due date.
  • Penalties: late filing or late payment penalties, if any.
  • Amount due: what you need to pay, and by when.

Can you reduce your payments on account?

Yes, if you expect your income this year to be lower than last year. You can ask HMRC to reduce them in your online account, or on form SA303. Only reduce them if you're confident – if your final bill turns out higher, HMRC charges interest on the difference.

If you can't pay

  • Pay what you can – interest and penalties are worked out on what's still unpaid.
  • Set up a payment plan (Time to Pay). You can often do this online if you owe £30,000 or less, or call HMRC on 0300 200 3820.
  • Don't ignore debt letters. If HMRC's Debt Management team writes to you, contact them before the date on the letter – HMRC can take enforcement action.

Questions

Why is my January tax bill so high?

On 31 January you pay the balancing payment for last year plus the first payment on account for this year. In your first year in Self Assessment, that can be up to one and a half times your tax bill.

What is a payment on account?

An advance payment towards next year's tax, normally half of last year's bill, due on 31 January and 31 July.

Can I set up a payment plan with HMRC?

Yes. Many people can set one up online if they owe £30,000 or less. Otherwise, call HMRC on 0300 200 3820. Do it as early as you can.
