Companies House strike-off notice: how to stop your company being closed

If Companies House thinks a company has stopped trading – usually because the accounts or confirmation statement are overdue – it starts to remove it from the register. Act quickly: once a company is dissolved, its bank account is frozen.

Why you got this letter

Companies House starts compulsory strike-off when it has reason to believe a company is no longer trading – most often because documents are overdue and its letters have had no reply. Usually two letters are sent, then a notice is published in The Gazette saying the company will be struck off.

What happens if you do nothing

  • About 2 months after the Gazette notice, the company is struck off and dissolved.
  • The company's bank accounts are frozen.
  • Its money and property pass to the Crown.
  • Getting it back means applying to restore the company, which costs time and money.

How to stop it

  1. File the overdue documents – usually the confirmation statement and any overdue accounts. Once they're filed, Companies House normally stops the strike-off.
  2. If you need more time, contact Companies House straight away. Anyone with an interest in the company can object; send the objection as early as possible, and at least 2 weeks before the Gazette notice runs out.
  3. After an objection, Companies House sets a deadline. If there's no progress by then, the strike-off starts again.

To file, you'll need the company number and the authentication code (a 6-character code sent to the registered office). Directors may also need to verify their identity with Companies House.

Late filing penalties too

Overdue accounts also mean a Companies House late filing penalty, and a late company tax return means a separate HMRC penalty. More about Companies House penalties.

Questions

How long before my company is struck off?

Usually about 2 months after the notice is published in The Gazette, if the overdue documents aren't filed and no one objects.

How do I stop a compulsory strike-off?

File the overdue documents as soon as possible – usually the confirmation statement and accounts. If you need more time, contact Companies House and object before the Gazette notice runs out.

What happens to the bank account if the company is dissolved?

It is frozen, and the company's money and property pass to the Crown. To get them back, the company has to be restored to the register.
