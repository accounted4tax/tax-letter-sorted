HMRC late payment penalty for Self Assessment: 5% explained

If tax from your Self Assessment return is still unpaid 30 days after the payment deadline, HMRC charges a penalty of 5% of the unpaid tax – and again at 6 and 12 months.

Key facts

Payment deadline
31 January after the end of the tax year
30 days late
5% of the tax still unpaid
6 months late
Another 5% of the tax still unpaid
12 months late
Another 5% of the tax still unpaid
Interest
Charged on top, from the due date – interest can't be appealed

What the letter means

The notice (form SA370, headed late payment penalties) shows the tax that was unpaid on a given date and the 5% charged on it. Page 2 usually shows the exact figure HMRC used.

The penalty is separate from any late filing penalty. You can owe both for the same year.

Interest is different

HMRC also charges interest on tax paid late, from the day after it was due until it is paid. Interest is not a penalty, so it can't be appealed – but if a penalty is cancelled, the interest on that penalty goes too.

If you can't pay

Not having the money is not a reasonable excuse on its own. What helps:

  • Pay what you can now – the 5% penalties and interest are worked out on what is still unpaid.
  • Set up a payment plan (Time to Pay). You can often do this online if you owe £30,000 or less, or call HMRC on 0300 200 3820.
  • Had a payment plan before the penalty date? Contact HMRC about the penalty, as it may not apply.

Can you appeal?

Yes, if a reasonable excuse stopped you paying on time and you paid as soon as you could. Examples HMRC gives include a serious illness or unexpected hospital stay, a death in the family shortly before the deadline, failure of HMRC's online systems, and fire, flood or theft. A payment that bounced because there wasn't enough money usually doesn't count.

See the full list on GOV.UK.

How to appeal

  1. Appeal within 30 days of the date on the penalty notice (later appeals need a reason for the delay).
  2. Appeal online, or post form SA370 to: Self Assessment, HM Revenue and Customs, BX9 1AS.
  3. Late filing and late payment penalties for the same tax year can go on the same SA370.

This page covers tax years up to 2025-26. From 2026-27, people in Making Tax Digital for Income Tax come under new penalty rules.

Questions

How much is the HMRC late payment penalty?

5% of the tax still unpaid 30 days after the deadline, then another 5% of what is unpaid at 6 months and again at 12 months. Interest is charged on top.

Is not being able to afford it a reasonable excuse?

No, not on its own. Set up a payment plan with HMRC as early as you can instead.

Can I appeal the interest?

No. Interest can't be appealed. If the penalty itself is cancelled, the interest on the penalty is removed as well.
