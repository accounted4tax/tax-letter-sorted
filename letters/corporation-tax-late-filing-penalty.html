Corporation Tax late filing penalty: the new £200 penalties explained

If your company tax return (CT600) reaches HMRC after the filing deadline, HMRC charges the company a penalty. For returns due on or after 1 April 2026, the fixed penalties have doubled.

This letter is from HMRC, not Companies House

Companies send two different things each year: the company tax return to HMRC, and the annual accounts to Companies House. Each has its own deadline and its own penalty. If your letter is from Companies House, see Companies House late filing penalty.

When it gets more serious

If the return is 6 months late (18 months after the end of the accounting period), HMRC may estimate the company's tax bill (a tax determination) and charge a tax-related penalty of 10% of the unpaid tax. It rises to 20% if the return is not delivered within 2 years of the end of the period.

A tax determination can't be appealed – the only way to replace it is to file the real return and pay the tax.

Can the company appeal?

Yes, if the company had a reasonable excuse and filed as soon as it could. Typical examples are the serious illness or death of the director who deals with the return when no one else could file it, a failure of HMRC's online services, or a fire or flood that destroyed records. HMRC usually expects other directors to step in, so the illness of one director is weaker where there are others.

How to appeal

  1. File the return and pay the tax first – HMRC may not deal with the appeal until it has both.
  2. Appeal within 30 days of the date on the penalty notice.
  3. Use HMRC's online appeal form, then print it and post it to: Corporation Tax Services, HM Revenue and Customs, BX9 1AX. You can also write to the address on the penalty notice.
  4. A director (or the company secretary) signs it. Include the company name, Corporation Tax reference (UTR) and the accounting period.

If HMRC refuses, the company can ask for a review or appeal to the tax tribunal within 30 days.

Questions

How much is the Corporation Tax late filing penalty in 2026?

For returns with a filing date on or after 1 April 2026: £200 if the return is late, another £200 at 3 months, and £1,000 or £2,000 if it is the third late return in a row.

Is the HMRC penalty the same as the Companies House penalty?

No. They are separate penalties from separate bodies, for different documents. A company can get both for the same year.

Can we appeal before filing the return?

You can send the appeal, but HMRC may not deal with it until the return is filed and the tax paid, so file first.
