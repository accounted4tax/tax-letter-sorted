Guides

£100 late filing penaltyHMRC charges a fixed £100 penalty when your Self Assessment tax return arrives after the deadline. It applies even if you owe no tax, or paid your tax on time. Daily, 6 and 12-month penaltiesIf your Self Assessment return is more than 3 months late, HMRC adds more penalties on top of the £100: £10 a day, then 5% or £300 at 6 months, and again at 12 months. Late payment penaltyIf tax from your Self Assessment return is still unpaid 30 days after the payment deadline, HMRC charges a penalty of 5% of the unpaid tax – and again at 6 and 12 months. Corporation Tax penaltyIf your company tax return (CT600) reaches HMRC after the filing deadline, HMRC charges the company a penalty. For returns due on or after 1 April 2026, the fixed penalties have doubled. Companies House penaltyCompanies House charges a penalty when a company's annual accounts arrive after the deadline. It is not HMRC – and it is harder to appeal than an HMRC penalty. Nudge letters (online sellers)A 'nudge' letter is HMRC asking you to check your tax, because it has information that you may have income you haven't told it about – often from selling online. It is not a fine and not an investigation, but don't ignore it. P800 tax calculationA P800 is HMRC's end-of-year check of the tax you paid through your job or pension. It tells you whether you paid too much – and get a refund – or too little, and how you'll pay it back. Simple Assessment (PA302)A Simple Assessment is a tax bill HMRC works out for you, without you filing a tax return. It usually comes when you owe tax that can't be collected through your tax code. Notice to file a tax returnA notice to file means HMRC wants a Self Assessment tax return from you for the tax year shown. Once you've been sent one, you have to file – even if you think you owe no tax. Self Assessment statementA Self Assessment statement shows what you owe HMRC and what you've paid: the balancing payment, payments on account, interest and any penalties. Many people are surprised by the payments on account. VAT penaltySince 2023, each VAT Return sent late gets a penalty point. When you reach your threshold, HMRC charges a £200 penalty – and another £200 for every late return after that. PAYE penaltyHMRC charges employers a penalty when payroll reports – the Full Payment Submission (FPS) – reach HMRC late, or when a month with no payments isn't reported. The amount depends on how many employees you have. CIS penaltyIf you're a contractor in the Construction Industry Scheme, your monthly CIS return must reach HMRC by the 19th of the following month. If it's late, HMRC charges a penalty that grows the longer it stays late. Making Tax DigitalMaking Tax Digital (MTD) for Income Tax means sole traders and landlords keep digital records and send HMRC an update every quarter, then a tax return after the year ends. HMRC writes to people who have to start. Strike-off noticeIf Companies House thinks a company has stopped trading – usually because the accounts or confirmation statement are overdue – it starts to remove it from the register. Act quickly: once a company is dissolved, its bank account is frozen.
